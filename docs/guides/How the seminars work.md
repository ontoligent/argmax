
The idea is to have a structured conversation that builds on itself over time.

To accomplish this, we following a process for each meeting:

1. **Do the reading**. Typically this is a paper or a collection of selections.
2. **Give a presentation**, ideally the author of the paper being discussed.
3. **Have a discussion**. This is a question-and-answer dialog involving the author or advocate of the ideas being presented in the seminar group.
4. **Produce and collect responses**. These are short to mid-length responses that capture one's thinking about the topic based on the seminar's conversation as well as the context of previous meetings and readings. Readings and writings will be hosted on this site.

Unlike a lecture series, participants come in with a prior knowledge of what is being presented and with questions. In addition, these questions build on previous seminars, and operate within a theme.
