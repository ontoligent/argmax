
The idea is to have a structured conversation that builds on itself over time.

1. Do the reading
2. Facilitator, presenter, and scribe
3. Presentation
4. Questions
5. Short responses

Unlike a lecture series, participants come in with a prior knowledge of what is being presented and with questions. Moreover, these questions build on previous seminars, and operate within a theme.

We are going to be using a document tool for this purpose of capturing and representing what we have done. 

Designate a discussion leader, a presenter, and a notes taker.
