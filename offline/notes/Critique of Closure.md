## Closure loop mapped onto the 4+1 areas				

Synthesis of the closure paper and Alvarado's 4+1 model; the mapping is an interpretation, not stated by either document.				

Step	Closure loop element	4+1 area(s)	Realm	How it plays out
1	Environment Eₜ	Value / Practice	Concrete humanity (Value); all four (Practice)	The human world where projects are motivated and whose state determines how data are generated.

2	Data Dₜ ∼ P(·|Eₜ)	Design + Systems	Abstract humanity; concrete machinery	Design represents the world as data (the private representation *of* the world); Systems ingests and aggregates it.

3	Analytic state Mₜ	Analytics	Abstract machinery	Models, parameters, and inference; where statistical validity lives.

4	Action Aₜ	Systems → Design → Value	Machinery back to humanity	Results are deployed (Systems), communicated as a public representation *for* the world (Design), and applied (Value).

5	Eₜ₊₁ = f(Eₜ, Aₜ)	Value / Practice	Concrete humanity	The world, now changed by the action, generates the next round of data. Closure = strength of dependence of Eₜ₊₁ on Aₜ.
