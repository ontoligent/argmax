Welcome everyone to the first meeting of the seminar ... 

Tonight we are delighted to have Tyler Cody and Peter Beling present their recent unpublished paper on Closure ...

Before getting started, let me quickly say a couple of things about the format of this seminar:

- First, the main idea is to have a structured conversation that build on itself over time.
  
- Unlike a lecture series, participants come in with a prior knowledge of what is being presented and with questions. 

- Moreover, these questions build on previous seminars, and operate within a theme. 

- At the end of each seminar, we will collect any comments and notes, including the transcript, and put them into a content management system.

- For this purpose, I am using something call Zensical, a markdown-based static site generator that works with GitHub pages.

- See https://ontoligent.github.io/argmax/
- https://ontoligent.github.io/argmax/seminars/Foundations%20of%20Data%20Science/syllabus/ 

- This will be an ongoing record of proceedings, notes, queries, and anything else relevant to the conversation.

- Finally, to make it work, each week there will be **a discussion leader**, a **presenter**, and, ideally, a **scribe** who will take notes.

- If anyone wants to be a scribe, just take notes and sent them to me afterwards and I will incorporate them into the site.

Finally, and without further ado, let me turn things over to Tyler Cody and Peter Beling to present.

- The format will be that they will summarize their argument in 20 to 30 minutes, and then open it for questions.
- Of course, we may ask questions along the way if it makes sense to do so ...
